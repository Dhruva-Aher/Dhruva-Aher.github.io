About Me

I go by Dhruv. I'm a Computer Science student at Arizona State University (4.0 GPA, Dean's List) in the Grand Challenges Scholars Program — building software at the intersection of backend systems and applied AI.

I grew up in Abu Dhabi, UAE, though my roots are in Mumbai, India — a city famous for its food, its chaos, and being the cinema capital of India. I spent most of my school years in Abu Dhabi, learned to drive at 17, and spent a lot of time playing badminton and cruising around the city with friends.

My path into CS started in grade 11, when I entered my first robotics competition. Something clicked. The idea that you could build something that physically moved and responded — using nothing but logic and code — made me want to go deeper. That curiosity hasn't stopped.

I work primarily in Python and Java, writing REST APIs, backend services, and full-stack systems. I care about performance, clean architecture, and code that behaves reliably — not just in demos.

Recently built a Raspberry Pi wearable using computer vision that achieved 90%+ accuracy identifying clothing attributes for visually impaired users, and shipped a full-stack AI productivity assistant in 36 hours at SunHacks 2025.

Long term, I want to build things that matter — and eventually find my way back to Mumbai.

Seeking Software Engineering Internship opportunities for Summer 2026 — backend or full-stack. Open to work.

About Me

I go by Dhruv — a Computer Science student at Arizona State University maintaining a 4.0 GPA on the Dean's List. I'm part of the Grand Challenges Scholars Program, where I focus on building software at the intersection of backend systems and applied AI.

I grew up in Abu Dhabi, UAE, though my roots trace back to Mumbai, India — a city known for its incredible food, relentless energy, and being the cinema capital of the country.

My journey into Computer Science began in grade 11 when I entered my first robotics competition. The idea that you could build something that physically moved and responded — using nothing but logic and code — sparked a curiosity that hasn't stopped since.

I work primarily in Python and Java, building REST APIs, backend services, and full-stack systems. I care about performance, clean architecture, and code that holds up in production — not just in demos.

Recently, I built a Raspberry Pi wearable using computer vision that achieved 90%+ accuracy identifying clothing attributes for visually impaired users, and shipped a full-stack AI productivity assistant in 36 hours at SunHacks 2025.

Long term, I want to build technology that makes a meaningful impact — and eventually find my way back to Mumbai.
